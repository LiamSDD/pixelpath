# Code of conduct

pixelpath adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full text at that link is the policy; this page says how it applies here.

## Scope

It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions and the security advisory threads, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Our standards

Be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, accept feedback on the work gracefully and give it about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults or derogatory comments, sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks, and publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission are not acceptable, in public or in private.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately through GitHub's private reporting for this repository: open the [Security tab](https://github.com/LiamSDD/pixelpath/security/advisories/new), choose **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with `Conduct:`. The report is visible only to you and the maintainer. Include links to the comments or threads involved. For a single abusive comment you can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on it.

There is no email contact; GitHub private reporting is the only channel.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report, replies within 7 days, and keeps the reporter's identity private. Responses follow the Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on the impact.
